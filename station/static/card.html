HamNet Station: voice net to text

Side 1 · Getting around

Open the dashboard

  • On the station PC: the HamNet Station icon on the desktop, or http://localhost:8091.
  • From a phone or another PC at home or on Tailscale:
    http://:8091
  • The station starts by itself when you sign in to Windows. It works with no internet.

What you see

One column per radio. On a phone, one radio at a time: tap its name under the header. A radio's dot turns green while it is receiving, red for emergency traffic.

The top is three tabs. On the air, the log, always fills the space below.

The three tabs

RadioFrequency, meters, waterfall and the buttons for this radio.
What you can do nowStep-by-step help when someone asks for a relay, calls priority or emergency traffic, opens a net or passes traffic. Also your 10-minute ID reminder. A dot means there is advice; a red dot means urgent.
Stations heardOne line per callsign: name, city, state, country. Tap a line for licence, grid and address.

More room for the log

  • ▴ Hide at the end of the tabs, or tap the open tab: the panel folds away and the log takes the screen. ▾ Show brings it back.
  • Tap a net's title to fold that net. Each browser remembers both.
  • Wide screen: drag the bar under the tabs to resize; double-click it to reset.

Reading the meters

RXLit while a station is transmitting.
Signallevel is good · low or hot · clipping, or almost no audio (check cable and level). The tick is the peak.
SNRWhile receiving: 20 dB or more · 10–20 dB · under 10 dB, expect mistakes.
WaterfallThe radio's audio over time. Voice is a bright smear; hiss is an even haze.

Before, during and after a net

Side 2 · Running a net

Before the net

  1. Tune the radio. If the frequency changed, press Change on the Radio tab and enter the new frequency and mode. Every transmission is logged with it.
  2. Check that Signal moves with traffic and stays green.
  3. Schedule the net once with Nets…: name, radio, days, start time, minutes, frequency, mode and net control's callsign. Naming net control lets the station mark its words.
  4. Optional, per radio: Listen only during scheduled nets. The radio starts 1 minute before each net and stops 5 minutes after it ends, so nothing in between is recorded.

During the net

  • Watch On the air. Each net gets a header with its time, frequency and how many stations took part.
  • If the speaker is missing or wrong, tap Who?, pick a callsign from the line or type one. Automatic undoes it.
  • Watch the What you can do now dot. Red means emergency or priority traffic: open the tab for the steps and phrases to use.
  • Your own station ID is due every 10 minutes and at the end of a contact (FCC §97.119). The tab reminds you.

Test transcription…Measure accuracy (see right).
SettingsTop of the page: your callsign, name, grid, and which Winlink Express sends.

After the net: Winlink messages

  1. Wait for the net header to say ✓ Accurate text for all. At home this is usually a few minutes after the net goes quiet. In the field there is no accurate pass: send with the live text.
  2. Press Winlink messages… on the net's header. It builds an ICS-309 communications log and an ICS-213 summary for that net only.
  3. Check the To: list. Stations the callbook can't confirm start unticked. Use Only callbook-confirmed, or add any callsign.
  4. Read and edit the messages.
  5. Close Winlink Express, then press Put in Winlink Express Outbox.
  6. Open Winlink Express and connect. The messages go out with your next session.

If the accurate pass finishes while the window is open, it offers Load the accurate text.

Measure accuracy

Press Test transcription…, have someone read the printed text over the air, then press Score. You get the share of words wrong, for the live text and the accurate pass. Re-test after changing the radio, cable or levels, and compare the numbers.

When something looks wrong

No text appearsIs the radio Paused? Does Signal move? Is the radio's squelch open on traffic?
Signal red, clippingTurn the radio's data or audio level down.
Signal red, no audioCheck the cable, and that the radio is on the right band.
Lots of probably noiseSquelch too open, or interference. Raise the squelch a little.
Wrong callsignsUse Who?. Check the maybe chips. Low SNR means more mistakes.
Outbox refusedWinlink Express is still open. Close it and try again.
Page won't openSign in to Windows on the station PC; the station starts at sign-in.
