HamNet Station

Side 1 · Install and open it

HamNet Station listens to your radio and writes down every transmission as text, a few seconds after the station unkeys. It shows who is talking, looks up every callsign, coaches you through relays and traffic, and builds the ICS-309 and ICS-213 Winlink messages after the net. It runs on a Windows PC and works with no internet.

You need

  • A Windows 10 or 11 PC (64-bit) with about 6 GB free.
  • Internet for the first install only (about 2.5 GB, 10–20 minutes).
  • The radio's receive audio on a USB sound input of its own, for example a data jack cable to a USB sound card. Never the input VARA or another digital mode uses.

Quick install

Download
github.com/owner/ham-net-speech-to-text
Scan with a phone camera to see the page, then open the same address on the PC.
  1. On the PC, open the address above. Press the green Code button, then Download ZIP.
  2. Open the downloaded ZIP and press Extract all.
  3. In the extracted folder open install → windows, double-click install.cmd, and press Yes when Windows asks.
  4. Type your callsign. Press Enter to skip the QRZ.com question if you don't use QRZ.
  5. Wait for == Done. It lists the PC's sound inputs; note the name of your radio's input.

To update later, download again and run install.cmd again. Your settings are kept.

Add your radio (once)

  1. Open C:\ham-net-speech-to-text\config\station.yaml in Notepad.
  2. Replace radios: [] with your radio, using part of the input's name for device:
radios:
  - id: vhf
    name: Club VHF
    device: USB PnP Sound
    gate: carrier   # voice for SSB/HF
    freq_mhz: 146.940
    mode: FM
  1. Save, then sign out of Windows and back in. The station starts again with your radio.

Open it

  • On the PC: double-click HamNet Station on the desktop.
  • On your phone: press Open on phone at the top of the page and point the phone's camera at the code. The phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as the PC.
  • Help at the top opens this card.

How to use it

Side 2 · Running a net

Reading the log

Grey wordsRough text while the station is still talking.
Black textThe text, 2–3 seconds after they unkey.
Blue edge
improving…		Better text is on its way. Wait a little and the line updates by itself. Only on stations with an accurate pass set up.
K7ABCA callsign: tap it for the station's card. Underlined: corrected from a near miss. A dotted maybe: a guess to check.
Underlined wordA Q-code or proword, with its meaning below. Untick Explain radio terms to hide meanings.
▶ 4 sPlay that transmission.
probably noiseNo speech in it. Ignore it.

Who is talking

K7ABCBold: the station gave its own call.
≈ K7NETGrey: probably net control.
K7ABC ✎You chose it with Who?. Your choice wins; Automatic undoes it.
blankNot known. It never guesses.

Before the net

  1. Tune the radio. If the frequency changed, press Change and enter it.
  2. Schedule the net once with Nets…: name, days, start time, length, frequency and net control's callsign.
  3. Optional: Listen only during scheduled nets. The radio starts 1 minute before each net and stops 5 minutes after.

During the net

  • Each net gets its own header in the log: time, frequency, how many stations.
  • Fix a missing or wrong speaker with Who?.
  • Red dot on What you can do now: emergency or priority traffic. Open it for the steps.
  • Identify every 10 minutes and at the end of a contact (FCC §97.119). The tab reminds you.

After the net: Winlink messages

  1. If the net header shows a progress bar, wait for ✓ Accurate text for all. Otherwise go ahead.
  2. Press Winlink messages… on the net's header. You get an ICS-309 communications log and an ICS-213 summary for that net.
  3. Check the To: list. Stations the callbook can't confirm start unticked.
  4. Read and edit the messages.
  5. Close Winlink Express, then press Put in Winlink Express Outbox.
  6. Open Winlink Express and connect. They go out with your next session.

First time: set your name, grid and Winlink Express account in Settings.

Buttons on each radio

⏸ PauseStop listening until ▶ Resume.
Clear log…Clear the screen. Files stay on the PC.
Test transcription…Someone reads a printed text on the air; Score shows the share of words wrong. Re-test after changing cable or levels.
