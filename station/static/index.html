Settings

On a repeater or simplex net, a callsign licensed farther away than this, heard only once, that the two transcription passes did not read alike and that has no name or earlier check-in with it is shown struck through and not counted. Raise it for a linked repeater system.
Laptops run several times slower once the screen goes dark. “During nets and traffic” switches to full speed for a scheduled net, for every transmission (from its first moment) and for 10 minutes after the last one; the rest of the time your own power plan, screen and sleep settings apply, and the radio still listens.
Older recordings, day logs, net history, minutes and voiceprints are deleted (checked every 6 hours). Settings, radios, the net schedule and local names are always kept.
