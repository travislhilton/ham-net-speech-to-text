Record a Conversation

A conversation is anything a radio hears outside a scheduled net: a contact, a ragchew, a call on a simplex frequency. There is no Record button: a radio that is listening records everything it hears, writes it down a few seconds after each station unkeys, and keeps it as one conversation with its own log, minutes and Winlink messages.

A · Before you talk

1
The radio must be listening. Its card shows ⏸ Pause. If it shows ▶ Resume, press it"Paused until … net" means the radio listens only during nets (Nets → Schedule → Listening). Resume records now; the next net's start or end takes over again.
2
Tell the station the frequency. A radio with CAT (computer control) reads it itself. Otherwise press Change on the card and enter the frequency and modeOn a scheduled net's frequency during the net, it is filed as that net; anywhere else, as a conversation.
3
Check the level. The Signal meter moves when someone talks. Not moving, or red? Set level… on the card

B · While you talk

4
It appears at the top of the radio's log under On the airGrey words while a station talks; the final text 2–3 seconds after it unkeys. Who is talking is shown first; tap a call for the station's name, license and grid.
5
Fix as you go (optional): Who? for the wrong speaker, ✎ Fix for wrong words or a wrong callThe station learns from your fixes. ▶ plays any transmission.
6
Sidekick (optional): on Draft it writes what it would say; asked a question, it answers at a pause in the conversationIts own card: Sidekick, the AI assistant.

C · When you are done

7
End conversation on its header → End conversation again to confirmRecording stops (the radio is paused; its scheduled nets still resume it), and the accurate pass re-reads the whole conversation at once.
8
Or just stop talking. After 15 quiet minutes it moves to Completed by itself and the radio keeps listening
A new conversation starts after 15 quiet minutes, when the radio's frequency changes, when a scheduled net starts on it, or after a net closes. Your own side: a radio does not hear itself transmit, so your words are written down only when another radio at the station is on the same frequency.

After the Conversation

Find it

Today and yesterdayThe radio's log → Completed: one line each (time, Conversation, frequency, transmissions, stations). Tap it to open
Any dayNets → History → tick Include conversations → tap one
By word or callSearch (top, or press /) → Look in: conversations only. Show in context › opens it

Use it

1
Wait for ✓ accurate on its line: the accurate pass has re-read it with the better modelThe text on screen updates by itself.
2
Minutes: who took part, what was said, plans and action itemsDownload .md keeps a file; Copy as text pastes it into an email or log.
3
▶ on a transmission plays the recording · ✎ Fix corrects it
4
Winlink messages…: an ICS-309 communications log and an ICS-213 summary, addressed to every station heardCheck the To: list (unconfirmed calls start unticked) and the text. Close Winlink Express → Put in Winlink Express Outbox → open Express and connect.
Kept on this PC only. The text, the recordings and the minutes stay in the station's data folder (logs, recordings, history.db). Clear log… on a radio's card only clears the screen: nothing is deleted.

When something looks wrong

Nothing written downRadio Paused? Does the meter move? Does the squelch open on voice? Run Set level…
Filed under a netThe card's frequency was a net's during its time: press Change when you move off it
Split in two15 quiet minutes, or the frequency changed. Both parts are in History
Can't hear the radioCable in its speaker / earphone jack (a handheld): the speaker goes quiet. Windows Sound → Recording → the radio → Properties → Listen → Listen to this device. A data jack (FTM-150R) keeps the speaker on
Wrong call or speaker✎ Fix or Who? on that transmission
Stopped listeningEnd conversation paused the radio: press ▶ Resume
