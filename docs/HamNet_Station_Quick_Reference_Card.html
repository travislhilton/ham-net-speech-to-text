Install and Get Started

Writes down every transmission your radio hears, a few seconds after the station unkeys: who is talking, every callsign looked up, the net's log and minutes, and the ICS (Incident Command System) 309 and 213 messages for Winlink. Runs on a Windows PC, with no internet once installed.

Download pagehttps://github.com/travislhilton/ham-net-speech-to-text

A · Install (once, on a Windows 10 or 11 PC)

1
Download: open the download page (scan the code) → green Code → Download ZIPInternet is needed this first time: about 4 GB, 10–30 minutes.
2
Unblock, then extract: right-click the ZIP → Properties → tick Unblock → OK; then right-click → Extract All
3
Run the extracted folder's install\windows\install.cmd and press Yes"Windows protected your PC"? More info → Run anyway. It asks for administrator rights itself.
4
Type your callsign. Press Enter to skip the QRZ.com question. Wait for == DoneKeep the PC plugged in while it measures. It lists what would slow this PC and the sound inputs it can record from.

B · First steps (on the dashboard)

5
Open the HamNet Station shortcut on the desktopOr http://localhost:8091. On a phone: Open on phone → scan the code (same Wi-Fi or Tailscale).
6
Add your radio: Radios… → + Add a radio → name, sound input, audio from, mode, frequency → Save and restart the stationIcom IC-705: also its CAT (computer control) port, so the station reads and tunes the radio.
7
Set level… on the radio's card: follow its steps with the radio's volume and squelch
8
Add your nets: Nets → + Add a net: name, radio, start, length, frequency, how it repeats
Each radio needs its own sound input, never the one VARA or Winlink uses. Two identical adapters? Rename one in Windows: Settings → System → Sound → the input → Rename.
On a laptop: keep it plugged in and set the maker's fan profile to Performance. The station keeps the screen on and runs at full speed only while it transcribes, then puts your power plan back.
Optional, in Settings: your name, grid and Winlink account (for the messages) · a QRZ.com login · local names · an xAI key for Sidekick, a station assistant that drafts what to say and never transmits (its own card: Sidekick card in Settings).
To update: download again and run install.cmd again. Your settings stay.

Running a Net

Before the net

1
Tune the radio to the net. Frequency changed? Change on the cardA radio with CAT tunes itself for each of its nets and is put back after. A net counts only on its own frequency.
2
5 minutes before, a yellow alert says what to set. Tune, then Radio is set

Reading the log

Grey wordsRough text while the station is still talking
improving…Better text is on its way; the line updates by itself (the accurate pass)
K7ABC · ≈ K7NETWho is talking: bold = gave its own call; grey ≈ = probably net control; blank = not known
K7ABCTap for the station's card (licence, name, grid). K7ABC? = no callbook confirms it
▶ 4 sPlay that transmission
Who? · ✎ FixWrong speaker, wrong words or call: fix it. The station learns from your fixes
Net boardWho is in, heard once (ask again), traffic, net control's 10-minute ID. After the net: review check-ins ✓ ✎ ✗

After the net: Winlink messages

3
Net closed (or press End net): recording stops and the accurate pass re-reads the net
4
Winlink messages… on the net's header: an ICS-309 communications log and an ICS-213 summaryCheck the To: list (unconfirmed calls start unticked). Read and edit.
5
Close Winlink Express → Put in Winlink Express Outbox → open Winlink Express and connect
Identify every 10 minutes and at the end (FCC §97.119). Minutes, History and Search (press /) keep every net: attendance, announcements, actions, traffic.

When something looks wrong

No textRadio Paused? Does the meter move? Does the squelch open on traffic? Run Set level…
Meter red / noneToo loud: turn the volume down. None: check the cable and the radio's input in Radios…
Wrong net nameIs the card's frequency what the radio is on? Change it
Outbox refusedWinlink Express is still open: close it, then try again
Page won't openSign in to Windows on the PC (the station starts at sign-in). Phone: same Wi-Fi or Tailscale
