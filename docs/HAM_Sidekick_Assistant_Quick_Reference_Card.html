Set Up Sidekick

Sidekick listens with you. After each transmission it decides whether your station should speak and drafts exactly what it would say: a check-in, an acknowledgement, a short answer. It drafts only until you switch Send on (see the checklist below): you read the draft and decide.

A · The xAI key (optional: without it Sidekick uses its offline rules)

1
Create a key at console.x.ai → API Keys → Create API KeyAdd a little credit. Sidekick asks the model only on its turns, about 1–4 times a net (well under a cent each). ▶ Hear it costs a little per clip.
2
Settings → Station agent → paste the key → SaveIt is kept on this PC only, never shown again and never sent anywhere but xAI.

B · Turn it on

3
Settings → Station agent → tick Enable the station agent, then fill in:
On-air nameSaid after your call (default Sidekick). Not a phonetic word, number or procedure word (Echo, Portable, Break)
Your first nameSidekick names you as its control operator
Your check-in lineWhat it says to check in, e.g. "This is W9XYZ, Sam, Springfield, no traffic"
License classIt never drafts outside your phone privileges
Your regionYour state (and county). It answers a roll call by place ("Stations Colorado, check in now") only for your region; left empty, never
4
On each radio's card: Sidekick Off → DraftOff by default on every radio. Its card appears at the top of that radio's column.
5
Nets where it must not check in (members-, mobiles- or traffic-only): Nets → the net → Sidekick on this net → Never checks in
6
Its voice (optional): Settings → Sidekick's voice → pick one, press ▶Plays in this browser only, never on a radio. Without a key or internet, the PC's own voice is used.
Draft only is the default. A radio's PTT stays off until you allow it. Before the first Send, once, into a dummy load: 1 Radios… → Transmit: how it keys, Audio out, tick Allow transmitting, Save. 2 Tick a dummy load is connected, press Tone + ID: set Drive level until deviation is right (about 3 kHz on FM; SSB: ALC just touching), Save. 3 Phrase + ID: check your call and the audio. 4 Radio card: Sidekick Send. You stay at the PC; Esc stops any transmission.
Every draft is checked in code against FCC Part 97 before you see it: your call at the end and every 10 minutes (§97.119), says it is an automated assistant and names you (§97.113), no music, codes or business, no relayed messages (§97.115), silent during emergency or priority traffic (§97.101), inside your privileges (§97.301/305). A draft that fails shows Would not send with the rule.

Sidekick on a Net

The YOUR TURN chip (top of Sidekick's card on each radio)

ListeningNot its turn; the reason is shown ("check-ins called for mobile stations")
Check-ins open to youNet control opened check-ins to a station like yours: a check-in is drafted
CALLEDSomeone called your call or Sidekick's name: what they said, and a reply
Answer readyA question that needed thinking is answered; it waits for net control's "comments or questions?"
Checked in ✓Net control acknowledged you, or your own check-in was heard: it will not check in again

Buttons

What would you say now?A draft for this moment, any time
▶ Hear it 👍 👎The draft in Sidekick's voice (browser only); rate it
I checked in myselfYou checked in on the air: it stops offering a check-in. Not checked in undoes it
Mute for this netSilent until the net ends. Unmute undoes it
Chat & prepared…Private chat; prepared words and talks: ▶ Hear, approve, Send now or Arm ("present the topic")
SendOn the air after a 3 s countdown, you at the PC; Esc stops

When it speaks, and when it stays quiet

1
Checks in when check-ins are open to all, to your region, or to "anyone with or without traffic"
2
Stays quiet for traffic-only, maritime, mobile, member or EOC calls; "before I start taking check-ins"; a bare "please call now" whose start was not heard; anyone else's roll call by place
3
Silent on "break break", emergency or priority traffic, on every net, unless net control calls you

When something looks wrong

No Sidekick on a radioSettings → Enable the station agent; then Draft on that radio's card
Never answers a roll callSet Your region (Settings → Station agent)
Wants to check in againPress I checked in myself
Amber Offline rulesNo key or no internet: it checks in and acknowledges, no answers
Would not sendA Part 97 check stopped it: the rule is shown. Nothing to do
Wrong turn👎 it: the ratings show what to fix (a net can be replayed: tools.sidekick_replay)
